Questions Parents Ask

What style of ballet do you teach?

We teach the Royal Academy of Dance (RAD) ballet syllabus, so she builds strong technique through high-quality routines set to gorgeous music. Then we add creative work that suits her age: stories for the youngest, and guided improvisation and creative dance as she grows, based on the 16 Creative Movement Concepts.

Who teaches the classes?

Miriam Teller, an RAD-certified ballet teacher with over 10 years' experience.

Does she need any ballet experience?

No. Every class welcomes beginners, and we teach each girl from where she is.

What should she wear?

Comfortable clothes. A ballet skirt is advised, or any flared knee-length skirt (not long), with tights, leggings or shorts underneath: these are a must. A ballet outfit is optional. Footwear that isn't slippery, ideally ballet shoes. Hair tied back helps.

How does the trial class work?

The trial class is on Wednesday 2 December (22 Kislev), at the same time as her regular class. It costs ₪40, which comes off your first month if she joins. Pay by cash, Bit or bank transfer. Choose “Yes” for the trial class in the form below.

When are the terms?

This term runs every Wednesday from 16 December (6 Teves), right after Chanuka, until 14 April (7 Nissan), just before Pesach. There's no class on Purim (Wednesday 24 March / 15 Adar II). Enrolment opens on Wednesday 2 December (22 Kislev). We're planning a second term after Pesach, running into the summer.

Where are the classes?

We're centrally located in Rechavia, Jerusalem. We email you the exact address when you enrol. Our class for 4-year-olds is at the Ginot Ha'ir community center (registration through the community center).

Are classes only for girls?

Yes. All our classes are for girls, with evening classes for high school girls and for women.

How much does it cost?

From ₪220 a month, depending on the class length (see the price table above). Register by the end of Chanuka for the Founding Families price, kept for your whole first year. Sisters get 10% off.

How do I pay?

Monthly, before the first class of each month: cash, Bit or bank transfer. Whole term to Pesach up front: 5% off. Cheques for every month to Pesach: 5% off.

When can family come and watch?

At our last class before Pesach, on Wednesday 14 April (7 Nissan). It's a special presentation class: the girls will get to invite family and friends (women and girls only) to watch what they've learned, and we're planning to give each girl a certificate.

Is there a performance?

Not yet, but it's in the works! We're hoping to put on a performance after Pesach, before the summer. Stay tuned for details.
