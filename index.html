Come Dance With Us!

Creative Ballet Academy is a warm, happy place to learn real ballet. Every class mixes proper technique with music, stories and plenty of fun, so girls leave a little stronger, a little braver and smiling.

Why Girls Love It

Confidence

Every new step she masters is a small win. Week by week she stands taller and tries new things.

Friends & Fun

Small groups, games and lots of laughing. A class she'll look forward to every Wednesday.

Expression

Stories, music and her own ideas. She learns to say things through movement, her way.

Classes & Times

Small groups with limited places in each class. Classes are for girls, every Wednesday from 16 December at Abarbanel 23 (top floor), Rechavia. Our ages 3–5 class meets at the community center.

  • Ages 3–5

    At the community center

    A gentle, joyful first ballet class through stories, music and movement. Ask us for the day and time.

  • Grades 1–2 · Kita Alef–Bet

    4:30–5:15 pm

    Twirls, jumps and stories set to music. A happy first taste of real ballet.

  • Grades 3–5 · Kita Gimel–Hei

    5:15–6:15 pm

    Real ballet steps, balance games and short dances to learn and show off.

  • Grades 6–8 · Kita Vav–Ches

    6:15–7:15 pm

    Stronger technique, longer dances and plenty of room for her own ideas.

  • High school & adults

    7:30–9:00 pm

    A full ballet class for high school girls and women. Beginners very welcome.

How to Join

  1. Send the form

    Tell us your daughter's grade and which class you're interested in. It takes a minute.

  2. Come to the trial class

    Tuesday 1 December, before Chanuka. Tick the box in the form and we'll send you the time.

  3. We confirm her place

    We'll call or message you to confirm her place and answer any questions.

  4. Come and dance!

    Classes start Wednesday 16 December at Abarbanel 23, top floor. Comfortable clothes are fine to start.
