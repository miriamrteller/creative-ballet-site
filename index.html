Come Dance With Us!

Creative Ballet Academy is a warm, happy place to learn real ballet. Every class mixes proper technique with music, stories and plenty of fun, so girls leave a little stronger, a little braver and smiling.

Why Girls Love It

Confidence

Every new step she masters is a small win. Week by week she stands taller and tries new things.

Friends & Fun

Small groups, games and lots of laughing. A class she'll look forward to every Wednesday.

Expression

Stories, music and her own ideas. She learns to say things through movement, her way.

Questions Parents Ask

What style of ballet do you teach?

We teach the Royal Academy of Dance (RAD) ballet syllabus, so she builds strong technique through high-quality routines set to gorgeous music. Then we add creative work that suits her age: stories for the youngest, and guided improvisation and dance games as she grows, based on the 16 Creative Movement Concepts.

Does she need any ballet experience?

No. Every class welcomes beginners, and we teach each girl from where she is.

What should she wear?

A ballet skirt or a knee-length skirt (long skirts aren't suitable), with tights, shorts or leggings underneath. Ballet outfits and ballet shoes are preferred and very welcome. No ballet shoes yet? Please make sure her tights aren't slippery. Hair tied back helps.

How does the trial class work?

The trial class is on Tuesday 1 December, at the same time as her regular class. It costs ₪40, which comes off your first month if she joins. Pay by cash, Bit, bank transfer or cheque. Choose “Yes” for the trial class in the form below.

When do classes start?

Wednesday 16 December, right after Chanuka, and every Wednesday after that.

Where are the classes?

We're centrally located in Rechavia, Jerusalem. We email you the exact address when you enrol. Our class for 4-year-olds is at the Ginot Ha'ir community center (registration through the community center).

Are classes only for girls?

Yes. All our classes are for girls, and the evening class is for high school girls and women.

How much does it cost?

From ₪220 a month, depending on the class length (see the price table above). Register by the end of Chanuka for the Founding Families price, kept for your whole first year. Sisters get 10% off.
