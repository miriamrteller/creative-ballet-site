Questions Parents Ask

What style of ballet do you teach?

We teach the Royal Academy of Dance (RAD) syllabus, which builds strong technique to gorgeous music. We add creative work to suit her age: stories for the youngest, then guided improvisation and creative dance, based on the 16 Creative Movement Concepts.

Who teaches the classes?

Miriam Teller, an RAD-certified ballet teacher with over 10 years' experience.

Does she need any ballet experience?

No. Every class welcomes beginners, and we teach each girl from where she is.

What should she wear?

Comfortable clothes she can move in.

Essential:

  • Tights, leggings or shorts
  • Hair tied back
  • Ballet shoes (preferred), non-slip socks or bare feet

Also:

  • A ballet skirt, or any flared knee-length skirt (not long)
  • A long-sleeved leotard (optional)
How does the trial class work?

Wednesday 2 December (22 Kislev), at her regular class time. It costs ₪40, taken off the first payment if she joins. Choose “Yes” for the trial class in the form below.

When are the terms?

Every Wednesday from 16 December (6 Teves), right after Chanuka, until 14 April (7 Nissan), just before Pesach. No class on Purim (Wednesday 24 March / 15 Adar II). A second term is planned after Pesach, into the summer.

Where are the classes?

Wednesday classes are centrally located in Rechavia, Jerusalem. We email the exact address when you enrol. Mini Ballet (ages 3–5) is at Mishkenot HaUma and Ginot Ha'ir: see Mini Ballet above.

Are classes only for girls?

Yes. All our classes are for girls, with evening classes for high school girls and for women.

How much is it, and how do I pay?

₪60 or ₪70 a class (see prices above). Pay for the term up front with 5% off, or in 5 monthly payments before the first class of each month, by cash, Bit or bank transfer.

When can family come and watch?

At the last class before Pesach, Wednesday 14 April (7 Nissan). It's a presentation class: girls invite family and friends (women and girls only), and we plan to give each girl a certificate.

Is there a performance?

Not yet. We hope to put one on after Pesach, before the summer. Stay tuned.
