Questions, Answered

How far in advance should we book?

Two to three weeks is ideal, but get in touch even if it's sooner and we'll do our best.

Do you come to us?

Yes. We come to your home, hall or garden anywhere in Jerusalem. Outside Jerusalem, ask us and we'll add the travel to your quote.

What should the girls wear?

Comfortable clothes they can move in, with non-slip socks or bare feet. We bring the tutus, crowns, scarves and ribbons.
