Questions, Answered

How far in advance should I book?

Two to three weeks is ideal, but reach out even if it's sooner — we'll do our best to make it happen.

Do you come to us?

Yes. We bring the celebration to your home, hall, or garden anywhere in Jerusalem, Ramat Beit Shemesh, and the surrounding area.

How many guests can join?

Mini hosts up to 8 children. Classic and Deluxe host up to 12. Larger group? Add guests at ₪40 each.

What should the children wear?

Anything comfortable they can move in. We bring the dress-up magic.

Can you do a joint party for siblings?

Absolutely — a joint celebration is a lovely way to honour two children at once, and we're happy to help you plan it.
